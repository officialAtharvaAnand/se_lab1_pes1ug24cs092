# Complete Requirements Table

## Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional - Alert Routing | The system shall ingest a monitoring alert, determine the active primary engineer from the current rotation, and dispatch the incident through webhook, SMS, and email. | High | **Pass:** A valid alert creates one incident and all configured channels notify the scheduled primary engineer. **Fail:** No incident is created, the wrong engineer is selected, or a configured notification is omitted. | Ensures every inbound alert reaches the person currently responsible. |
| FR-002 | Functional - Escalation | The system shall escalate an unacknowledged P1 incident to each configured secondary tier after 5 minutes per tier until an engineer acknowledges it. | High | **Pass:** A test P1 alert advances to the next tier after each 5-minute timeout and stops immediately after acknowledgement. **Fail:** Escalation is late, skips a tier, continues after acknowledgement, or the incident is dropped. | Prevents critical incidents from remaining unattended. |
| FR-003 | Functional - Rotation Management | The Incident Commander shall create, update, and publish on-call rotations, including primary and secondary assignments, shift times, and temporary overrides. | High | **Pass:** A published schedule and override return the correct engineer at the shift boundary. **Fail:** Overlapping assignments are accepted without warning or an active override is ignored. | Provides an authoritative and flexible source for routing decisions. |
| FR-004 | Functional - Incident Coordination | The On-Call Engineer shall acknowledge an incident, update its status and timeline, and resolve it; the Incident Commander shall be able to assign or reassign ownership. | High | **Pass:** Authorized actions persist with actor and timestamp, and acknowledgement halts escalation. **Fail:** An unauthorized or unrecorded update succeeds, or escalation continues after acknowledgement. | Creates accountable, real-time coordination throughout the incident lifecycle. |
| FR-005 | Functional - Post-Mortem Tracking | The Incident Commander shall create and close a post-mortem linked to a resolved incident, recording impact, root cause, timeline, corrective actions, owners, and due dates. | Medium | **Pass:** Closure succeeds only when all mandatory fields and action owners are present, and the record remains linked to the incident. **Fail:** An incomplete post-mortem closes or loses its incident association. | Converts incident learning into traceable preventive action. |

## Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-001 | Performance & Availability | For 99.9% of alerts each month, the system shall create the incident and initiate every configured notification within 3 seconds of ingestion, while maintaining 99.95% monthly availability. | High | **Pass:** A peak-load test at 100 alerts/second records a p99 dispatch latency of at most 3 seconds, and monthly monitoring shows availability of at least 99.95%. **Fail:** Either threshold is missed. | Fast, dependable dispatch limits the time before response begins. |
| NFR-002 | Security & Auditability | The system shall enforce role-based access, encrypt data in transit with TLS 1.2 or later and at rest with AES-256, and retain tamper-evident audit records for schedule and incident changes for at least 365 days. | High | **Pass:** Security tests reject unauthorized role actions, verify the required encryption, and retrieve complete immutable audit entries throughout the retention period. **Fail:** Any unauthorized action succeeds, protected data is unencrypted, or an audit entry can be altered or is missing. | Protects sensitive contact and incident data while supporting accountability and compliance. |
